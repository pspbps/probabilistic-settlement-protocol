# How to Cite and Use PSP

This document defines how third parties may reference, cite, or implement the
Probabilistic Settlement Protocol (PSP).

---

## Canonical Reference

The canonical definition of PSP is maintained in this repository:

https://github.com/pspbps/probabilistic-settlement-protocol

Any reference to “PSP” or “Probabilistic Settlement Protocol” MUST refer to the
specification defined here, unless explicitly stated otherwise.

---

## Correct Usage

### Acceptable References

Implementers MAY state:

- “This system implements the Probabilistic Settlement Protocol (PSP).”
- “This contract is compatible with PSP v1.0.”
- “Settlement outcomes follow PSP probabilistic rules.”

---

### Required Clarifications

Implementers MUST clarify when applicable:

- The PSP version implemented
- Whether the implementation is modified or extended
- That the implementation is not authoritative unless explicitly stated

Example:

> “This application implements PSP v1.0 with custom extensions.  
> The PSP specification is defined at https://github.com/pspbps/probabilistic-settlement-protocol.”

---

## Prohibited Usage

The following statements are NOT permitted:

- “This project **is** PSP.”
- “This is the official PSP implementation.” (unless explicitly designated)
- “PSP guarantees profit or outcomes.”

---

## Forks and Derivatives

Forks of this repository:

- Must not claim canonical authority
- Must clearly rename or scope modifications
- Must not imply governance control over PSP

---

## Academic and Industry Citation

Recommended citation format:

> Probabilistic Settlement Protocol (PSP), Version X.Y  
> https://github.com/pspbps/probabilistic-settlement-protocol

---
